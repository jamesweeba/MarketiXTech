The terms for using MarketiX

Last updated: 19 November 2026

MarketiX Tech ("MarketiX", "we", "us") runs a marketplace on WhatsApp where market vendors sell foodstuff, buyers place orders, and affiliates earn by sharing shop links. These terms apply whenever you use MarketiX on WhatsApp, our vendor site or this website.

By placing an order, opening a shop or sharing an affiliate link, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use MarketiX.

1. Who can use MarketiX

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • The details you give us, such as your name, phone number and delivery address, must be correct.
  • You must not use MarketiX for anything unlawful or fraudulent.

2. Our role

The foodstuff on MarketiX is sold by independent market vendors. Each vendor sets their own products and prices and is responsible for the quality of what they sell. MarketiX provides the shop, takes the order, handles payment and arranges delivery.

3. Orders and prices

  • You order through a vendor's shop link, in a form that opens inside WhatsApp.
  • Prices are in Ghana cedis (GH₵) and are set by the vendor. The total you see before you place the order, including any delivery fee, is the amount you pay.
  • An order is accepted when we confirm it to you on WhatsApp.
  • Foodstuff is fresh produce and stock changes through the day. If an item you ordered is not available, we will tell you on WhatsApp and you can choose a replacement or have that item removed and refunded.

4. Payment

  • You can pay by mobile money (MoMo) when you order, or in cash or by MoMo when your order is delivered.
  • Mobile money payments are processed by the mobile money operator and our payment partners. We never see or store your mobile money PIN.
  • If you choose to pay on delivery, please be available to pay the full total when the order arrives.

5. Delivery

Delivery timeframe

  • Orders are delivered within 24 hours of the order being confirmed.
  • We deliver every day of the week.
  • Your order confirmation on WhatsApp tells you when to expect your delivery, and we message you when it is on its way.

Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses in the same town or city as the vendor's market. If we cannot deliver to your address, we will tell you before the order is confirmed, and any payment you have made will be refunded in full.

Receiving your order

  • Please give a clear address or landmark and keep your phone on, so the rider can reach you.
  • Check your order when it arrives.
  • If the rider cannot reach you at the address you gave, we will contact you on WhatsApp to arrange another delivery. A second delivery fee may apply.

Delays

Heavy rain, traffic or a problem at the market can sometimes hold up a delivery. If your order will not reach you within 24 hours, we will tell you on WhatsApp, and you can choose to wait or to cancel for a full refund.

6. Cancellations and refunds

Cancelling an order

You can cancel free of charge at any time before the vendor starts preparing your order. Message us on WhatsApp to cancel. Once the vendor has started preparing a fresh food order, it can no longer be cancelled.

Problems with an order

If an item is missing, wrong, damaged or spoiled, tell us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo where possible. We will replace the item or refund what you paid for it.

Because foodstuff is perishable, we cannot accept returns or give refunds because you have changed your mind after delivery.

How refunds are paid

Refunds for orders paid by mobile money are sent back to the number that paid, within 5 working days of the refund being agreed.

7. Vendors

  • Registering and opening a shop is free. MarketiX earns a 10% commission on purchases.
  • You must list your foodstuff and prices accurately, and keep your shop up to date when something runs out.
  • You must supply foodstuff that is fresh, safe to eat and matches what the buyer ordered, and have each order ready in time for delivery.
  • We pay you for completed orders to the mobile money details you gave us, less our commission and any refunds for items that were missing, wrong, damaged or spoiled.
  • We may suspend or close a shop that breaks these terms or that receives repeated complaints.

8. Affiliates

  • Commission is paid to the mobile money details you gave us.
  • You must not send spam, make false claims about a shop or its prices, or place orders through your own link only to earn commission. We may withhold commission and close affiliate links that are misused.
