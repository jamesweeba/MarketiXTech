FAQ

Questions people ask us

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp

What can I buy on MarketiX Tech?

Foodstuff from local market vendors. Open a vendor's shop link on WhatsApp to see what they have and their prices.

How do I order and pay?

The whole order happens in a WhatsApp Flow, a short form that opens inside the chat. You pick your items, say where to deliver them, then choose how to pay: by mobile money (MoMo) or on delivery.

Do I need a website or a new app?

No. Vendors run their shop and buyers place their orders inside WhatsApp, so there is nothing extra to build or download.

What does it cost to open a shop?

Nothing. Registering and opening a shop on MarketiX Tech is free. We earn a 10% commission on purchases instead.

How do I open a shop?

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us what you sell. We will help you set up your shop and get your shop link.

How do affiliates earn?

Share the link to a shop. When someone buys from that shop through the link you shared, you earn 10% of that purchase.

Do I need my own shop to be an affiliate?

No. Anyone can share a shop link and earn, whether or not they sell anything themselves.
