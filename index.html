Who it's for

MarketiX Tech brings market vendors onto WhatsApp, so the people who sell foodstuff, the people who buy it and the people who spread the word all meet in one chat.

Vendors

You sell in the market

Put your stall on WhatsApp. Register on our vendor site, upload your foodstuff and prices, and you get a shop with one link to send to your customers. Orders reach you complete: what they want, where it is going and how they are paying.

It costs nothing to register. We do not charge vendors to open a shop or to list their foodstuff.

Buyers

You need foodstuff

Open a vendor's shop link and the order form comes up right inside WhatsApp. Tick what you need, say where to bring it, and choose MoMo or pay on delivery. There is no app to install, no account to create and no long list to type out.

How an order works

  1. A vendor opens a shop

    A market vendor registers on the MarketiX vendor site and uploads the foodstuff they sell.

  2. The shop link gets shared

    Vendors and affiliates send the link to customers, friends and followers.

  3. The buyer orders in the flow

    The shop opens as a WhatsApp Flow, a short form inside the chat where the buyer picks their items.

  4. Delivery and payment

    Still in the flow, the buyer says where to deliver, chooses MoMo or pay on delivery, and places the order.

Common questions

What can I buy on MarketiX Tech?

Foodstuff from local market vendors. Open a vendor's shop link on WhatsApp to see what they have and their prices.

How do I order and pay?

The whole order happens in a WhatsApp Flow, a short form that opens inside the chat. You pick your items, say where to deliver them, then choose how to pay: by mobile money (MoMo) or on delivery. There is nothing to download.

What does it cost to open a shop?

Nothing. Registering and opening a shop on MarketiX Tech is free. We earn a 10% commission on purchases instead.

How do I open a shop?

Register on our vendor site, then upload your products and prices. If you get stuck, message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

How do affiliates earn?

Share the link to a shop. When someone buys from that shop through the link you shared, you earn 10% of that purchase.

Do I need my own shop to be an affiliate?

No. Anyone can share a shop link and earn, whether or not they sell anything themselves.
